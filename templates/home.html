Scout Law

A Scout is trustworthy, loyal, helpful, friendly, courteous, kind, obedient, cheerful, thrifty, brave, clean, and reverent.

Scout Oath

On my honor I promise to do my best to do my duty to God and my country, to help other people at all times, and to live by the Scout Law.
